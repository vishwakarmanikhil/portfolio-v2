A real estate website generates leads when it does two things well: it **answers the questions a buyer has before they call**, and it makes **enquiring take less than a minute**. A beautiful homepage is not enough. Buyers want to know where the land is, whether the documents are clear, and how to talk to someone.

I have built websites for several real estate firms selling plots and flats around Mumbai and Pune, including [Infinity Realty](https://infinityrealtypune.com/), [LandCity Properties](https://landcityproperties.com/) and [Prime Properties JD](https://primepropertiesjd.com/). This is the checklist I use.

## What do buyers look for on a real estate website?

Before a buyer fills in a form, they are trying to answer a few questions:

- **Where exactly is it?** Which village, taluka or area, how far from the nearest city, highway or station.
- **What can I buy?** Plot sizes, flat types, farmhouse or investment plots.
- **Is it legally clear?** Which approvals and documents exist, and whether someone will explain them.
- **Can I trust this company?** Past buyers, real photos, a real office and phone number.
- **What does it cost, roughly?** Even a starting price or range helps people decide whether to enquire.

If the website answers these, the people who enquire are already interested. That is a better lead than someone who calls only to ask "where is this?"

## Which pages should a real estate website have?

For most firms I build these pages:

1. **A page for each location or project.** Each one describes the place, plot or flat types, nearby landmarks, photos and a map. Separate pages also rank better on Google for searches like "plots near Pune" or "plots in Navi Mumbai".
2. **Plot or property types.** What sizes and options exist, and who each one suits (weekend home, farmhouse, investment).
3. **Legal and documents.** Plain-language explanations of approvals and paperwork. Buyers are nervous about land titles, and a page that explains them before booking builds trust quickly.
4. **Testimonials and photos of real buyers.** Sale-deed handovers, site visits and families on their plots are more convincing than stock photos.
5. **FAQs.** Real questions buyers ask on calls: road access, water, electricity, registration, payment plans.
6. **Contact.** Phone, WhatsApp, office address and an enquiry form on every page.

## How do you make enquiring easy?

The enquiry form is where leads are won or lost. What works:

- **Keep the form short.** Name, phone number, what they are interested in, and an optional message. Every extra field loses people.
- **Put it everywhere.** A form or "Enquire now" button on every location page, not only on the contact page.
- **Offer WhatsApp.** Many buyers prefer a WhatsApp message to a phone call.
- **Make it fast on phones.** Most real estate visitors browse on mobile data. Large images that load slowly cost you enquiries.

## What happens after someone enquires?

This is where many real estate businesses lose money. Enquiries arrive in an email inbox or a WhatsApp group, nobody is sure who called back, and some leads are never contacted at all.

Every real estate client I work with now uses [Enquiro](/projects/enquiro), the enquiry management system I built for this problem:

- Every website enquiry lands in **one admin panel**.
- The admin **allocates enquiries** to sales employees.
- Each employee sees **only their own enquiries**, adds comments after each call, and **updates the status**.
- The admin can **filter and export** enquiries to Excel at any time.

Whatever tool you use, the rule is the same: every enquiry should have one person responsible for it, and you should be able to see what happened to it.

## How do you get found on Google and AI assistants?

Buyers search for things like "farmhouse plots near Pune" or "CIDCO-approved plots in Navi Mumbai". To show up:

- Give each location its **own page** with a clear title and description.
- Make sure the text is in the page itself, not only in images or brochures.
- Add **structured data** and a **sitemap**, so Google understands your pages.
- Answer common questions on the page. AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Perplexity quote websites that answer questions directly.

## A quick checklist

- [ ] A page for each location or project, with photos and a map
- [ ] Plot or property types explained
- [ ] Legal and document guidance in plain language
- [ ] Real testimonials and photos
- [ ] FAQs from real buyer calls
- [ ] A short enquiry form and WhatsApp on every page
- [ ] Fast on mobile
- [ ] One system where every enquiry is tracked until it is closed
