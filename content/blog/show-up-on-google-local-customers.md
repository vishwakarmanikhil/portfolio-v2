When someone searches "plots near Pune", "CA firm in Thane" or "car service near me", Google shows two things: a **map with a few businesses**, and a **list of websites**. To be found by local customers, you need to show up in both. That takes two things working together: a complete **Google Business Profile** and a **website with the right pages**.

None of this needs a big budget. It needs the basics done properly, then kept up to date.

## What is a Google Business Profile?

Your Google Business Profile is the free listing that appears on Google Maps and in the map section of search results. It shows your name, category, address or service area, phone number, hours, photos and reviews.

For many local businesses, it brings in more calls than the website itself. If you haven't claimed yours, start at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com/).

## How do you set up your profile properly?

Most profiles are created and then forgotten. A complete one does much better:

1. **Choose the right primary category.** This is one of the strongest signals for which searches you appear in. Pick the most specific one that fits ("Real estate developer", not just "Company").
2. **Use your real business name.** Don't stuff keywords into it. Google can suspend profiles for that.
3. **Add your address or service area,** phone number, website and opening hours, and keep them current.
4. **Add real photos**: your office, your team, your work, your projects. Add new ones regularly.
5. **Write a clear description** of what you do and where.
6. **List your products or services.**
7. **Post updates** occasionally: new projects, offers, events.

## Why do reviews matter so much?

Reviews affect both whether you appear and whether people choose you. To build them honestly:

- **Ask every happy customer** right after a good experience. Send them the direct review link from your profile.
- **Reply to every review**, good or bad, politely and quickly. People read your replies.
- **Never buy fake reviews.** Google removes them, and customers can usually tell.

## What does your website need for local search?

Your website is what Google checks to confirm what you do and where. Make it easy:

- **A page for each service and each area you serve.** "Residential plots in Navi Mumbai" deserves its own page, with real details, not one sentence on the homepage.
- **Your name, address and phone number on the site,** written exactly as on your Google Business Profile.
- **Local details in the text**: areas, landmarks, distances, how to reach you.
- **Structured data** (schema.org `LocalBusiness`) that tells Google your business details in a machine-readable format.
- **A fast, mobile-friendly site.** Most local searches happen on phones.
- **Text in the page itself,** not only inside images or PDFs, so Google can read it.

## How do you know if it's working?

Two free Google tools tell you:

- **Google Business Profile performance**: how many people saw your profile, called, asked for directions or visited your website.
- **[Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)**: which searches show your website, how often people click, and any problems Google finds on your pages.

Check them once a month. If people see your profile but don't call, improve your photos, reviews and description. If your website appears in searches but few people click, improve your page titles and descriptions.

## How long does it take?

Your profile can start appearing within days of being verified. Ranking well for competitive searches takes longer, often months of steady work: reviews, photos, updates and useful pages on your site. There is no shortcut, and anyone who guarantees a "number one ranking" is not being honest with you.

## A quick checklist

- [ ] Google Business Profile claimed and verified
- [ ] Specific primary category, real name, correct hours
- [ ] Real photos, added regularly
- [ ] A steady habit of asking for, and replying to, reviews
- [ ] A website page for each service and area
- [ ] The same name, address and phone number everywhere
- [ ] Search Console set up and checked monthly
